Our approach

Reputation first, sales second

Chinese players are quick to try new games, and they lean heavily on what creators and other players say. Impulse buys without the right expectations often end in negative reviews. So we build reputation before launch day.

KOL influence

Creators show real gameplay, so players can see what they're getting.

Media authority

Proof it's good

Coverage from respected gaming media gives the game a quality signal.

Perception

Advocacy
